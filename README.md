# MACROPAD-FALLOUT
# WHAT IS MY PROJECT
____________________
This is an custom Hollow Knight Macropad with keys bound to its controls
It is unique because hollow knight is tough with controller and a pair of keyboard and mouse which creates a delay in pressing controls but this solves that problem
# WHAT IT DOES 
---------------
It makes hollow knight a little bit more handy and easy
hollow knight is tough with controller and a pair of keyboard and mouse which creates a delay in pressing controls but this solves that problem 
and makes the gaming experience 10 times non rage baiting

# WHY IT EXISTS 
----------------
It exists because I am a fellow Hollow Knight player and I am stuck at 94% in game even after acheving the true ending and defeating the final boss and to achieve that 112% or even 100% mark I must defeat Newer DLC bosses and Pantheons which are insanely tough and its a pain to die because of delay in controls
